Clipper Uploader

Clipper Uploader is a personal tool that cuts short highlight clips from videos its owner is authorized to use, and uploads each clip to the owner’s own YouTube channel as a private video. The owner then reviews every clip in YouTube Studio before deciding whether to publish it.

What it does

  • Transcribes a source video and picks the strongest moments.
  • Reframes them for vertical viewing and adds captions.
  • Uploads the finished clips to the owner’s YouTube channel, as private videos.

Access to Google data

The app asks for one permission: uploading videos to the signed-in user’s YouTube channel (youtube.upload). It cannot read, change, or delete anything else on the channel. How the app handles data is described in the Privacy Policy.
